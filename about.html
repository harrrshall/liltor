Security

Encryption, access, residency, vendor chain.

Publish PCI, SOC 2, or ISO 27001 only with reports in the trust packet. Ask ujjwal@liltor.xyz for the current packet. This page lists controls. The packet holds the reports.

Encryption

TLS 1.2 or newer in transit. AES-256 at rest for call audio, transcripts, and rooftop config. Keys stay in the cloud account named on your order form.

Access control

Role-based access for advisors, fixed-ops, and group IT. SSO is on the Group plan. Production audio is off limits to anyone without a named rooftop role.

US residency options

Audio and transcripts can stay in US regions when the order form says so. Group deals write the region before go-live.

Vendor chain

Privacy

Call audio is customer data

Recordings, transcripts, repair-order identifiers, and phone numbers collected while an agent is on the line belong to the rooftop customer. Liltor processes that data to run and adapt agents for that rooftop.

What we collect on this site

The walkthrough form collects your name, rooftop, work email, phone, lane interest, and message. We use it to schedule a walkthrough. It stays with the walkthrough inbox.

Deletion

Deletion path within 30 days of a written request, legal holds excepted. Write ujjwal@liltor.xyz from the rooftop’s named email and specify audio, transcripts, form data, or all three.

Adaptation

Handoff audio is used to adapt the agent for that rooftop. It is not used to train a public demo voice. Group deals can write a tighter retention window on the order form.

Terms

The service

Liltor provides production voice agents for dealership operations: inbound and outbound calling, service scheduling, parts status, and BDC appointment setting, with human handoff. Subscription fees are per rooftop as on the order form.

Rooftop and term

A rooftop is one physical store with one service book. Annual is typical. Usage caps and overage are the numbers printed on the order form.

Audio license

You grant Liltor a license to process call audio and transcripts solely to operate, evaluate, and adapt agents for your rooftops. That license ends when the agreement ends, subject to the deletion path and legal holds.

Handoff and booking

The agent writes appointments only on the books you configure. You remain responsible for hours, loaner policies, pricing, and the people who take handoffs. Vehicle deals stay with the desk. Card data stays off this site.

Acceptable use

Do not use the agents to record a call where your consent path is missing, to impersonate a person who does not work at the rooftop, or to route emergency services. Keep posted after-hours messages accurate.
