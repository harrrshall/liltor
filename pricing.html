Order-form questions

Term?

Annual is typical. Monthly rooftop rates above convert on a twelve-month order. A shorter term is a conversation on the walkthrough.

Numbers?

Porting is a project we schedule. DIDs, hunt groups, and after-hours routing land on a dated cutover, not a self-serve toggle.

Carrier?

Telephony partners sit under Liltor’s agreement. You keep your posted numbers. The rooftop keeps a single carrier contract for the agent path.

What counts as a rooftop?

One physical store with one service book. A group of four stores is four rooftops, even when they share a DMS tenant.

Overage?

Caps are written as included minutes and included outbound attempts. Overage is a unit price on the same form, printed with the caps.
